— Our Story

Two food cultures, one kitchen in Kuilsrivier.

Riyaad grew up helping at his oupa’s koesister stall on weekends. Farah trained as a chef in Cape Town, then spent two years cooking in Mexico City — where she couldn’t stop thinking about how well those street tacos would sit next to her ma’s curry.

Taco Karnal started as a fold-out table at the Kuilsrivier community market in 2020. Two years and hundreds of test recipes later, they opened a small corner shop on Van Riebeeck Road — and people started driving in from Bellville and Kraaifontein just for a taste.
